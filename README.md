# **MY FAVORITE MOVIE**
## Bugonia(2025)
### Psychological thriller starring Emma Stone as Michelle Fuller, a powerful CEO who is kidnapped by two men convinced that she is an alien sent to destroy Earth. They believe that exposing her true identity will save humanity. As the story unfolds, the line between reality, conspiracy, and madness becomes increasingly blurred, leading to a shocking ending.

# **CLIMAX OF THE MOVIE**
### Highlights of the movie
1. The shocking kidnapping – Two conspiracy theorists kidnap Michelle, a powerful CEO, because they believe she is an alien.

2. Emma Stone's performance – She delivers a powerful and unsettling performance, showing fear, intelligence, manipulation, and emotional strength.

3. The psychological battle – Michelle and her kidnappers engage in a battle of intelligence, as neither side knows whom to trust.

4. The conspiracy theories – The movie makes viewers question whether the men are mentally unstable or whether their suspicions have some truth.

5. The unexpected twists – Just when you think you understand what's happening, the story takes a surprising direction.

6. The shocking ending – The final scenes reveal a disturbing truth that changes how you understand the entire movie.
